# Managing user profile

You can view and update your user profile on the APEX portal.

## Updating user email address

Update your contact email address to ensure that you receive any important announcements such as downtimes or portal changes.

To update your email, follow these steps:

1. Click the **Profile** icon on the upper right corner of the portal.

1. Click **View Profile**.

1. Click **Update Email**. 

1. Enter your updated or valid **email address**.

1. Click **Submit**.
